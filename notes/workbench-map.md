# The workbench map

Moved on 2026-09-29. The map of what the desk's windows consume, produce
and save, with its drawings, is a document in the notebook now:
`docs/nes/workbench.md`, served at `/docs/nes/workbench` and listed on the
notebook's "The desk" shelf. It is tracked here (the one file under
`docs/nes/` that is), because the desk is this repository's; everything
else under `docs/nes/` is pulled from the sibling checkouts at build time.

`notes/workbench.md` remains the 2026-09-22 inventory (what exists, what
is missing) and `notes/modules.md` maps every module edge out of the
repository.
